To help us update your contact details, please provide a copy of an identity document with a recent photo of you.

For example:

 - your application registration card (ARC)
 - your biometric residence permit
 - a Home Office travel document
 - your national passport
 - an official letter from the Home Office with your photo on it
 - your UK driving licence (photocard only)
 - your EEA national identity card
 - an immigration bail notice (BAIL.201 or IS.248) which shows your photo
