You cannot use this form if you already have a decision on your asylum claim.

## What to do instead

### If you want to appeal a decision

You need to either:

- sign in to your <a class="govuk-link" href="https://manage-case.platform.hmcts.net/">MyHMCTS account</a>
- contact the <a class="govuk-link" href="https://www.find-court-tribunal.service.gov.uk/courts/immigration-and-asylum-appeals-service-centre">Immigration and Asylum Appeals Service Centre</a>

If you have not started an appeal yet, you can do this online or by post. Find out more on how to <a class="govuk-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/immigration-asylum-tribunal/overview">appeal a visa or immigration decision</a>.

### If you are a refugee

You can update <a class="govuk-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/evisa/update-ukvi-account">your details on your eVisa online</a>.

### If your claim has been withdrawn

You can request a review or challenge the decision. More information is available in the <a class="govuk-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/withdrawing-asylum-applications/withdrawing-asylum-claims-accessible#bookmark34">Withdrawing asylum claims</a> guide.
