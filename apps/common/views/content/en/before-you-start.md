You might find it helpful to get some information or documents ready before you start.

To find your claim we will ask for your contact details as they appear on your claim now, including:

 - your name
 - your date of birth
 - your unique application number (UAN) – you can find this number on Home Office letters

You might also need to upload documents to prove what you want to update. These should be translated into English.
 
Examples could be:

 - a marriage certificate showing your new name
 - a birth certificate for a new baby
 - a photo identity document, like an Asylum Registration Card
 - a letter from the person claiming asylum saying you can help them
 - a document sent to you by the Home Office

If you need to stop at any time to find a document or take a break, you can save the form, exit and come back later. We will send you a link so you can return to the form when you are ready.
