You cannot use this form if you already have a decision on your asylum claim.

## What to do instead

### If you want to appeal a decision

You need to either:

- sign in to your [MyHMCTS account](https://hmcts-access.service.gov.uk/enter-email)
- contact the [Immigration and Asylum Appeals Service Centre](https://www.find-court-tribunal.service.gov.uk/courts/immigration-and-asylum-appeals-service-centre)

If you have not started an appeal yet, you can do this online or by post. Find out more on how to [appeal a visa or immigration decision](https://www.gov.uk/immigration-asylum-tribunal/overview).

### If you are a refugee

You can update [your details on your eVisa online](https://www.gov.uk/evisa/update-ukvi-account).

### If your claim has been withdrawn

You can request a review or challenge the decision. More information is available in the [Withdrawing asylum claims](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/withdrawing-asylum-applications/withdrawing-asylum-claims-accessible#bookmark34) guide.
